# Code of Conduct

Redfilade brings together people who attack systems so that those systems can be made safe. That only works if the community itself is safe to be part of, and if everyone's skills point in the same ethical direction.

## Our standards

We expect everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space) to:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to security or to MCP.
- Critique ideas, code and attacks, never people.
- Give credit for others' findings and work.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a contribution, and ask why rather than argue.

These are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or sexualised language or imagery
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing conversations
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Pressuring someone to disclose a vulnerability before it is fixed

## Ethical conduct

Because Redfilade is an attack platform, we add rules that a general community would not need:

- **Never use Redfilade, or anything learned here, against systems you do not own or are not authorised to test.**
- Do not post working exploits for unpatched vulnerabilities in third-party software in public project spaces. Disclose them privately to the affected maintainers first.
- Do not submit attacks, scenario packs or targets that try to harm the people who review or run them, beyond what their manifest honestly declares.
- Report security flaws in Redfilade itself privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Breaking these rules is treated as seriously as harassment, and may be reported to the affected parties.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose conduct is harmful.
